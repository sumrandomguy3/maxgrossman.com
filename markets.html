Markets

Where to find me

Most of what I make gets sold in person, across a handful of markets and street fairs around Essex County. Come pick something up — the spoons in particular are made to be held, and that's hard to do through a screen. If you're thinking about a commission or have a piece that needs restoring, the booth is a good place to start that conversation.
